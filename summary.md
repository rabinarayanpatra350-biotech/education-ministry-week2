# Executive Summary — Week 2: Core Responsibilities & Tasks

**Education Ministry Internship, Coimbatore** | Rabi Narayan Patra | 27 September 2026

Building on Week 1's map of the education administration system, this report documents the intern's actual duties across the six assigned areas, grounded in verified national statistics.

## Key numbers

- **RTI 2023-24**: 17.51 lakh requests, 3.86% rejected, 1.77 lakh first appeals (CIC Annual Report)
- **CPGRAMS 2025-26**: 18.34 lakh grievances received, 18.13 lakh disposed, average disposal 14 days against the 21-day prescribed limit (Lok Sabha Q. 4034)
- **National Scholarship Portal 2024-25**: 21.55 lakh applications, 18.69 lakh verified, ₹2,558.91 crore disbursed across 143 schemes
- **Samagra Shiksha PAB approvals for Tamil Nadu**: ₹3,586 crore (2024-25) → ₹2,734 crore (2025-26) → ₹2,469.4 crore (2026-27); ~₹3,440 crore of the Centre's committed share pending, RTE reimbursements of ₹362.8 crore released following a Madras High Court order
- **PGI 2.0 (2023-24)**: Tamil Nadu 559.2/1000 (Akanshi-1), learning outcomes 41.2/100 the weakest domain; no State in the top grades; national gap narrowed from 51% to 42%
- **UDISE+ 2023-24**: 14.72 lakh schools, 24.8 crore students, 98.08 lakh teachers, 19.7 crore students Aadhaar-seeded
